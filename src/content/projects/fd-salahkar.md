- **AI advisor chat** grounded in real FD information through a RAG pipeline, with quick questions and tap-to-explain jargon like tenor, maturity, TDS and DICGC
- **Bank comparison** across 15 banks, filtered by government, private or small finance, and sorted by rate, safety or minimum deposit
- **Maturity calculator** with sliders for amount, tenure and rate, plus monthly, quarterly or annual compounding
- **Open FD flow**: a guided four-step booking, from choosing a bank to confirming
- Works in **Hindi, Tamil and Telugu**
