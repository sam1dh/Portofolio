- **Public blog** with search and categories: Technology, Hackathons, Research and Clubs
- **Post pages** with comments and related posts
- **Admin dashboard** showing total blogs, comments and drafts, with publish and unpublish controls
- **Rich-text editor** for writing posts with thumbnails and subtitles
- **JWT-based admin authentication** on a Node.js, Express and MongoDB backend
- **Related-post recommendations** using TF-IDF and cosine similarity
